# Minit Charger — Complete NEW Brand Design Theory

**Project:** Full website renewal (Webflow Core + Site Premium · Maxero theme base)  
**Sources:** [minitcharger.com](https://www.minitcharger.com/) · [az-maxero.webflow.io](https://az-maxero.webflow.io/) · client product imagery (`product-images/`)  
**Audience:** B2B industrial buyers only — **no consumer pricing / e-commerce**  
**Design horizon:** 2026 — industrial-futuristic, premium, acquisition-led

---

## 0. Executive verdict

Minit Charger is **not** a consumer EV wallbox brand. It is a **25-year industrial motive-power charging + fleet intelligence company** serving warehouses, airports, cold storage, ports, and manufacturing.

The purchased **Maxero** theme gives the correct *layout grammar* (modular sections, soft premium UI, navy contrast, FAQ → CTA). We keep that structure and **replace** Maxero’s consumer EV story, purple identity, and pricing funnel with Minit’s industrial authority, charge-green system, and quote-led sales motion.

**One-line brand promise**

> Charge faster. See everything. Keep the fleet moving.

## 4. NEW visual system (2026 industrial-futuristic)

### 4.1 Design principle

**Maxero’s structure + Minit’s industrial product truth.**  
Feel like a 2026 premium industrial energy site: crisp product renders, deep navy planes, charge-green signals, soft mist panels, intentional motion—**not** purple SaaS, not cream-serif lifestyle, not glow spam.

### 4.2 Color tokens

Map Maxero purple slots → Minit **Electric Charge** green. Keep navy / mist / white surfaces.

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--mc-charge` | `#5CBF4A` | Primary CTA, links, icons |
| `--mc-charge-deep` | `#3F9A32` | Hover / pressed |
| `--mc-charge-soft` | `#ECF8E9` | Soft success tint (sparingly) |
| `--mc-navy` | `#0B1220` | Dark sections, footer |
| `--mc-navy-mid` | `#151D2E` | Elevated dark panels |
| `--mc-ink` | `#111827` | Body on light |
| `--mc-mist` | `#F3F1F8` | Soft lavender-grey fills (theme) |
| `--mc-white` | `#FFFFFF` | Cards, panels |
| `--mc-line` | `#E5E7EB` | Hairlines only when needed |
| `--mc-safety` | `#E8B923` | Optional data/UI accent (from product screens)—never primary brand |

**Avoid:** Full-bleed lime trays from old site · Purple primary · Cream+serif “AI default” · Multi-layer glow stacks · Rounded-full pill clusters as decoration.

### 4.3 Typography

| Role | Family | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Display / H1–H2 | **Space Grotesk** | Geometric, 2026 tech presence |
| Body / UI | **Plus Jakarta Sans** | Readable, premium, not Inter |

- Hero ~48–64px · Section titles ~32–40px · Body 16–18px · LH ~1.6  
- Sentence case headlines; small uppercase labels only for section eyebrows  

### 4.4 Shape & UI (from Maxero + 2026 polish)

- `rounded-2xl` / `rounded-3xl` / `rounded-4xl` on cards & media  
- Dual CTAs: solid charge primary + outline secondary  
- Soft white cards on mist; dark storytelling on navy  
- Subtle mesh / grid atmosphere behind hero (not the main visual)  
- Icons: simple line / duotone in charge green  
- Glass only as thin utility (nav blur)—not glassmorphism overload

### 4.6 Motion (ship 2–3 intentional moves)

1. Hero media + copy fade/slide-up on load

No floating badges on hero media. No glow spam.

### 4.7 Voice & SEO (acquisition)

**Voice:** Confident, operational, specific. Short sentences. Measurable claims.

**Primary keywords**

- industrial battery charger  
- forklift battery charger  
- GSE charger / eGSE charging  
- material handling charging  
- lithium lead-acid AGM charger  
- fleet charging management / AssetPro 360  

**H1 pattern:** Outcome + category  
Example: *Industrial rapid charging for fleets that can’t afford downtime.*

**CTAs**

- Primary: **Get a Quote**  
- Secondary: **Explore Solutions** / **Talk to an Expert** / **Request AssetPro Demo**

**Never:** Consumer plan pricing · “Shop” as primary · Fake Login product unless a real portal ships.
