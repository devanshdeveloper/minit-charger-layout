Frequently asked questions

Straight answers for fleet, facilities, and procurement teams evaluating industrial charging systems.

Still have questions? Contact us
What types of batteries are compatible with Minit Charger? +

Our chargers support lithium, lead-acid, and AGM batteries—ideal for mixed industrial fleets migrating chemistries over time.

How fast can a depleted battery recharge? +

Typical configurations recharge a fully depleted battery in about three hours, depending on battery type, capacity, and operating conditions.

Are products suitable for outdoor use? +

Yes. Solutions such as Altus II are designed for outdoor eGSE applications, with weather-minded construction and dual-port performance for apron operations.

Can AssetPro 360 integrate with our existing systems? +

AssetPro 360 is built to connect with enterprise workflows, including ERP and CMMS paths, so charging and asset data can live alongside the tools your teams already use.

How do we buy—is there online pricing? +

Minit Charger sells through a consultative process: we scope your fleet and site constraints, tailor the configuration, then provide a transparent quote and installation plan. There is no consumer self-serve pricing.
