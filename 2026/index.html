Operational challenges

Why industrial fleets switch to smart rapid charging.

Forklift battery chargers, GSE chargers, and fleet charging management only pay off when they cut downtime, protect batteries, and fit mixed chemistries.

Charger-related downtime

When material handling equipment sits waiting on a slow or failed charge, shift plans slip. Rapid industrial battery chargers with on-screen diagnostics keep MHE and GSE moving.

Mixed battery chemistries

Fleets migrating from lead-acid to lithium often run both. Minit Charger supports lithium, lead-acid, and AGM so you standardize hardware without forcing a one-chemistry rip-and-replace.

No visibility into battery health

Without CellTrac and AssetPro 360, maintenance reacts after failures. Cloud fleet charging management surfaces SoC, alarms, and charger status before equipment goes dark.

Outdoor GSE harsh conditions

Airport apron and port yards need outdoor-rated eGSE chargers. Altus II is built for dual-port outdoor duty with remote diagnostics for ground support fleets.

Multi-site standardization

3PL and distribution networks need one industrial charging stack across warehouses. Configure Maximus, Momentus, and AssetPro once, then scale the same playbook site to site.

CapEx without clear ROI

Procurement needs a consultative quote—not consumer pricing pages. We scope fleet size, duty cycles, and install constraints so CapEx maps to measurable uptime outcomes.

Glossary

Industrial EV charging terms explained.

MHE
Material handling equipment—forklifts, pallet jacks, reach trucks, and similar warehouse vehicles that rely on industrial battery chargers.
GSE / eGSE
Ground support equipment (and electric GSE) used on airport aprons—baggage tractors, belt loaders, and pushbacks needing outdoor-rated chargers.
Opportunity charging
Fast partial recharge during breaks instead of waiting for a full conventional charge cycle—common with lithium fleets and rapid industrial chargers.
SoC
State of charge—how full a battery is. CellTrac and AssetPro 360 track SoC so fleets avoid unexpected dead units mid-shift.
BMS
Battery management system—onboard monitoring of charge, discharge, alarms, and health. CellTrac is Minit Charger’s smart BMS module.
AssetPro 360
Cloud fleet charging management platform for chargers, batteries, analytics, alerts, and ERP/CMMS-connected operations.
