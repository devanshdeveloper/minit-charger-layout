Frequently asked questions

What types of batteries are compatible? +

Our chargers support lithium, lead-acid, and AGM batteries — ideal for mixed industrial fleets.

How fast can a depleted battery recharge? +

Typical configurations recharge a fully depleted battery in about three hours, depending on battery type and configuration.

Are products suitable for outdoor use? +

Yes. Altus II is designed for outdoor eGSE applications with weather-minded construction and dual-port performance.

Can AssetPro 360 integrate with our systems? +

AssetPro 360 connects with enterprise workflows including ERP and CMMS paths.

Is there online pricing? +

No consumer self-serve pricing. We scope your fleet, tailor the configuration, then provide a transparent quote and install plan.
