FAQ

Answers before you ask.

Which battery chemistries do your chargers support?

All Minit Charger systems support lithium, lead‑acid, and AGM batteries, with charge profiles tuned per chemistry to protect battery health.

Can outdoor units handle extreme climates?

Yes. Altus II and other outdoor‑rated units are built for exposure to heat, cold, and coastal environments across ports and airport ramps.

What does AssetPro 360 actually monitor?

Charging status, equipment performance, utilization analytics, and proactive fault detection — all in one cloud dashboard with customizable alerts.

How long does installation take?

Most sites move from signed quote to live install within a few weeks, depending on port count and site electrical readiness.

Do you support mixed fleets across multiple chargers?

Yes — Altus II, Maximus, Momentus, and Durus can run side by side on one AssetPro 360 account for full mixed‑fleet visibility.
